Chicken Adobo

Description

Chicken Adobo is a classic Filipino dish made by braising chicken in a savory, tangy sauce of soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and black peppercorns.

It is known for its rich flavor profile and long shelf life, tasting even better the day after it is cooked.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken thighs and drumsticks
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/3 cup white vinegar
  • 1 whole head of garlic, crushed
  • 1 teaspoon whole black peppercorns
  • 3 dried bay leaves
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil

Steps

  1. Combine chicken, soy sauce, and crushed garlic in a bowl. Marinate for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a large pot over medium heat. Brown the chicken pieces on all sides, reserving the marinade.
  3. Pour the remaining marinade, bay leaves, and peppercorns into the pot. Bring to a boil.
  4. Lower the heat, cover, and simmer for 25 minutes until the chicken is tender.
  5. Pour in the vinegar and let it simmer uncovered for 10 minutes without stirring to cook off the raw acid taste.
  6. Serve hot with steamed white rice.
