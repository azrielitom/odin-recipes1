Pancit Canton

Description

Pancit Canton is a flavorful stir-fried egg noodle dish tossed with sliced meats and crisp vegetables in a savory soy-citrus sauce.

Traditionally served at birthdays and celebrations to symbolize long life and good health.

Ingredients

  • 250g dried Pancit Canton flour noodles
  • 150g chicken breast, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup cabbage, chopped
  • 1 medium carrot, julienned
  • 1/2 cup snap peas
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 2 cups chicken broth

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a large wok and sauté the garlic until fragrant.
  2. Add the sliced chicken and cook until no longer pink.
  3. Toss in the carrots, snap peas, and cabbage. Stir-fry for 2 minutes so they stay crunchy, then remove the veggies and set aside.
  4. Pour the chicken broth and soy sauce into the same wok and bring to a boil.
  5. Add the dried noodles and toss gently until the noodles absorb all the liquid and soften.
  6. Return the cooked vegetables to the wok, toss everything together, and serve with calamansi wedges.
