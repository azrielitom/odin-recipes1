Pork Sinigang

Description

Pork Sinigang is a comforting, sour tamarind-based soup loaded with tender pork ribs and fresh local vegetables.

The sour broth balances the richness of the pork, making it a staple meal especially during rainy days.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pork belly or spare ribs, cut into serving pieces
  • 1 packet tamarind soup base (Sinigang mix)
  • 2 medium tomatoes, quartered
  • 1 medium onion, quartered
  • 1 radish (labanos), sliced
  • 1 bunch water spinach (kangkong)
  • 2 pieces long green chili (siling haba)
  • 8 cups water

Steps

  1. Boil water in a large pot and add the pork, onions, and tomatoes.
  2. Simmer over medium-low heat for 45 to 60 minutes until the pork is fork-tender.
  3. Add the sliced radish and simmer for 5 minutes.
  4. Stir in the tamarind soup base and green chilies. Taste and adjust seasoning with fish sauce (patis).
  5. Add the kangkong leaves last, turn off the heat, and cover for 2 minutes until the leaves wilt.
  6. Serve immediately while piping hot.
